All four signatures are `finalized` with no error (`getSignatureStatuses`). The fee payer spent about 0.00008 SOL in total.

Two things the first, unpaced attempt showed:
- The public devnet RPC rate-limits by IP. Fifteen calls in a few seconds exhausted it for the firewall (blockhash) and the facilitator alike; later calls were refused before signing (`payment creation failed`). For volume, give the firewall and the facilitator their own RPC endpoint.
- Under that rate limit the facilitator answered 402 for two payments that had in fact landed on chain (payer 20.00 → 19.96 USDC for three "delivered" calls). The firewall logged them as undelivered and kept counting them, which is the safe side, but the seller's answer and the chain disagreed.
