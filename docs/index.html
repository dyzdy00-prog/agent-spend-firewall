Watch a number. Hear only when it matters.

A watch buys one paid answer again on a schedule and compares it with the last look. You say what counts: any change, a line the number should not cross, or a move in percent. Every look is a payment through the firewall, counted against that agent's budget. The transcripts below were recorded on 21 and 23 Nov 2026 against the practice sellers, where no money moves.

Name one field of the answer and a line. The watch remembers only which side of the line the number is on, so you are told when it crosses, not every time it moves, and again only after it has come back.

No seller takes the whole budget

most to one seller · per hour, per day

An hourly budget refills every hour. A loop, or a seller that splits one thing into many paid calls, can drain it again and again. A limit per seller counts all your agents together, by the hour and over any 24 hours.

One payment, five checks, a few milliseconds.

The firewall never trusts a description of a payment. It reads the exact bytes it is about to sign.

1
The agent calls the tool

fetch_paid_resource(url) over MCP, with its token. It has no key to send.

2
The firewall fetches

The seller answers HTTP 402 with a price. Redirects and internal addresses stop here.

3
It reads the bytes

Amount, asset, recipient, expiry: decoded from what would be signed.

4
It checks your rules

Cap, budgets, payee, validity, replay and delivery history, in one atomic step.

5
It signs, or it doesn't

One signature for what passed. Every decision goes to the ledger.
